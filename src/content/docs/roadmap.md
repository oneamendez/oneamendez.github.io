---

Speed comes first. Every VM runs on the
[crate engine](https://github.com/oneamendez/crate), so the engine's speed is the
whole app's speed, and that's where help counts most.

## 1. Speed

Faster translation, startup and graphics in the engine, measured by real
benchmarks: timings of what people actually do, such as booting to a desktop,
opening apps and building code, on the iPhone itself, so every change shows what
it gains. Today's numbers are on [Engine and speed](/crate/engine/).

## 2. Monitors and devices

An external monitor already works as the VM's screen. Next, the keyboards, mice,
game controllers, cameras, microphones, USB storage and other devices iOS lets an
app reach, passed through to the VM; the other way, the VM's sound, clipboard and
files reaching iOS.

## 3. More systems

Omarchy back in the catalog once its bugs are fixed, custom Linux images of your
own, then other operating systems.
