---
title: External monitor
description: Use a monitor as the VM's screen, with the iPhone or iPad as its trackpad.
---

Connect a monitor to the iPhone or iPad while a VM runs, and the VM's screen
moves to the monitor at the monitor's own resolution, and the iPhone or iPad
becomes the desktop's trackpad.

## If the desktop doesn't fill the monitor

- **GNOME (Ubuntu)**: open Settings › Displays inside the VM and pick a
  resolution with the monitor's aspect ratio, such as 1920 × 1080 for a 16:9
  monitor.
- **Xfce**: the panel, icons and windows follow the new size, but the wallpaper
  keeps its old one for now.

See [Known issues](/guides/known-issues/) for both.
