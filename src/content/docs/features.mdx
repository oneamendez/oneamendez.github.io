## Real distributions, from a catalog

The Install tab lists Ubuntu 26.04 LTS with GNOME, Debian 13 and Arch Linux ARM
with KDE Plasma or Xfce. Pick one, a memory size and a disk size, and crate
downloads it, checks every file, and asks for the account to create in it. The
images are each distribution's own, built and attested by a public GitHub
workflow.

## Memory and disk of your choice

A memory limit from 2 to 12 GB, which you can change later in the VM's
settings, and a disk from 16 GB up, set at install. The disk is sparse: it takes
space on the iPhone only as the VM writes. If crate closes by itself, a smaller
VM fixes it: see [Tips](/crate/guides/tips/).
[Memory and disk →](/crate/guides/memory-and-disk/)

## GPU-accelerated

The guest's OpenGL runs on the iPhone's GPU through virglrenderer, ANGLE and
MoltenVK. Desktops composite in hardware, browsers accelerate canvas, WebGL and
WebGPU, and 3D apps render on the GPU.

On an iPhone Duo (A20 Pro), with OpenGL ES 3.1 at 952 × 608:

- **glmark2**, the standard Linux OpenGL benchmark, scores 1092 across four of
  its scenes: jellyfish 982 fps, ideas 1278, phong shading 1113, refraction 1001.
- **The WebGL Aquarium** in Chrome runs 500 fish at about 60 frames a second, the
  screen's refresh rate.

## A mouse or trackpad on iPad

A mouse or trackpad drives the desktop's pointer, with scrolling and right and
middle click, and a hardware keyboard types into the VM, its shortcuts included.

## An external monitor

The VM's screen moves to the monitor at the monitor's resolution, and the iPhone
or iPad becomes its trackpad.

## The serial console

Each VM's serial console is a tab away from its desktop, for when you want a
plain terminal, or to watch it boot.

## JIT, built in

crate turns on JIT itself with its built-in StikJIT, or with StikDebug if you
already use it. [Turning on JIT →](/crate/guides/jit/)

## Logs for bug reports

crate can log what it and the engine do, this launch and the last, kept across a
crash, with its memory use if you want. Export Logs shares them.
[Logs and bug reports →](/crate/guides/logs/)
